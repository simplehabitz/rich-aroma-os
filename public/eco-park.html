Why does MetaMask show wrong balances or fail?
On Ethereum & Solana, USDC is an ERC-20 token with 6 decimals. But on Arc Mainnet, Circle engineered USDC as the native L1 gas token, which uses standard EVM 18 decimals.

If MetaMask was configured with 6 decimals, MetaMask calculates amounts off by 1,000,000,000,000× (showing trillions of dollars or throwing "insufficient funds for gas * price + value").

🛠️ Step-by-Step 60-Second Fix:

1 Switch to Ethereum: In MetaMask, open the network picker at top left and temporarily switch to Ethereum Mainnet (or Arbitrum/Polygon).
2 Delete Old Arc Entry: In MetaMask, open Settings ⚙️ > Networks > Arc Mainnet and click Delete. (MetaMask requires deleting an existing network before it allows changing decimals).
3 Click Re-Add Below: Click "Add Arc Mainnet (18 Decimals)" below. MetaMask will prompt you to approve Arc with the exact 18-decimal configuration.
