The Quimistán Reality Check: Who actually buys and what can they afford?

In a municipality where the average daily wage is L. 250 – L. 350 ($10 – $14 USD), expecting 160 cups a day at $3.25 (81 Lempiras) from everyday locals is completely unrealistic. A viable Quimistán shop operates on a Two-Tier Model:
• Locals (50% of orders): Café con leche, drip coffee, baleadas, and sweet bread at L. 25 – L. 45 ($1.00 – $1.80).
• Highway Travelers & Weekend Remittance Spenders (50% of orders): CA-4 transit drivers, soccer players, and families celebrating with iced caramel coffees, frappes, and burritos at L. 65 – L. 110 ($2.60 – $4.40).
• Realistic Foot Traffic: 45–60 orders on slow weekdays; 85–110 on weekends/game nights (average ~65 tickets/day).
